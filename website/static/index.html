What Are Disposable Email Addresses?
And why are they a security concern?

Definition

Disposable (temporary) email addresses are self-destructing inboxes created by services like Mailinator, Guerrilla Mail, and 10 Minute Mail. They require no registration and expire after a short period.

Why They Are Risky

  • Cannot be traced back to a real identity
  • Commonly used to bypass email verification
  • Frequently seen in spam and phishing campaigns
  • No way to follow up or revoke access

30 Extracted Features

The system automatically extracts three groups of 30 features from the email address. Each feature is encoded as −1 (phishing indicator), 0 (suspicious), or +1 (legitimate indicator).

Top 3 Most Discriminative Features

#1
Known Legitimate Provider
Importance: 0.3199
Whether the sender domain is a well-known mail service (Gmail, Outlook, QQ, etc.). The single strongest predictor — phishing emails rarely come from trusted providers.
#2
Username Length
Importance: 0.2503
Phishing email usernames are often unusually long. Normal user-created usernames are typically under 30 characters.
#3
High-Traffic Mail Platform
Importance: 0.0708
Major platforms like Gmail and Outlook are strong legitimacy signals. Phishing domains are almost never ranked in any traffic index.

How It Works

A six-stage end-to-end machine learning pipeline.

01
Dataset Acquisition
UCI Phishing Websites Dataset (ID 327) — 11,055 labeled samples, 30 features. Downloaded via ucimlrepo with local CSV caching and a synthetic fallback generator.
02
Exploratory Data Analysis
Class distribution (44.3% phishing / 55.7% legitimate), zero missing values, full 31×31 correlation heatmap, and per-feature-group signal analysis.
03
Preprocessing
Label remapping (−1 → 0 phishing, 1 → 1 legitimate), stratified 80/20 split, StandardScaler fitted on training data only — no data leakage.
04
Model Training
Four classifiers: Logistic Regression, Random Forest (100 trees), SVM (RBF kernel), Decision Tree. All trained in under 3 seconds on an Apple M-series processor.
05
Email Feature Extraction
The 30 UCI structural features are mapped to email address analysis: domain reputation, keyword detection, username entropy, brand spoofing checks — all offline, no network calls required.
06
Real-Time Inference
FastAPI REST backend serves the trained Random Forest. Input any email address and receive an instant verdict with per-feature explanations and risk indicators.
